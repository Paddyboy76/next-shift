# Mission 08 — External Evidence and Verification Failure

Make evidence independence undeniable.

Adversarially verify:

- specialist claims alone cannot close work;
- missing evidence cannot close work;
- malformed or stale evidence cannot close work;
- evidence from the wrong capability cannot close work;
- verifier identity remains independent;
- evidence provenance is visible to operators/judges;
- failed verification remains inspectable and recoverable.

Evaluate and implement judge-visible Gemini vision-based operational evidence only if it is a genuine independent evidence source and preserves deterministic authority.

Vision evidence is high-value. Do not implement decorative image analysis.

No clinical imagery or clinical decisions.
